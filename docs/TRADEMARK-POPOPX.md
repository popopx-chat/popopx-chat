## Permitted contexts

- **Your own copy.** Running and describing an unmodified POPOPX Chat application that you obtained
  from POPOPX's own distribution channels.
- **Truthful derivation and compatibility statements.** "A fork of POPOPX Chat", "works with the
  POPOPX network", "connects to a POPOPX relay" — in prose, in documentation, in a repository
  description, as long as the words describe a relationship and are not used as the name or the
  branding of the software doing the describing.
- **Publications.** Articles, blogs, videos, talks and presentations about POPOPX Chat or the POPOPX
  network. Screenshots that contain POPOPX artwork are a separate permission —
  [ASSETS_LICENSE-POPOPX.md](../ASSETS_LICENSE-POPOPX.md).
- **Unofficial communities and events**, labelled "Unofficial".

## Prohibited contexts

- **Software name and branding, including forks.** No POPOPX mark as the name, branding, product
  name, package name, bundle identifier or logo of software that POPOPX did not publish. This binds a
  fork of this repository in full: to distribute a build you change the name and ship your own
  wordmark, icons and illustrations.
- **Implied affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement** — in a domain name, an app-store or F-Droid
  listing, a server banner, a relay's advertised operator name, or any "official" wording.
- **Domains and addresses** containing or resembling "popopx" where a reader would take it to be ours,
  including `popopx.*` registrations and SMP/XFTP addresses presented as POPOPX-operated.
- **Commercial activities.** Advertising, merchandise, promotions, or a service offered to the public
  under the POPOPX name, without prior written permission.
- **Any other use** not listed as permitted.

## Enforcement

Permission requests and questions: email **chat@popopx.chat**. Policy updates are posted here.

The remedy we ask for is the one that costs a fork nothing that AGPL-3.0 has not already given it:
rename the software, replace the artwork, keep the code. We act against confusion, not against forks.
